## Sugarlif® - Low GI Rice

Rice is one of the most vital ingredients in our daily food consumption and can become a challenge for rice lovers facing pre-diabtes or diabetes. The GI of normal rice is about 69 (which is high). Sugarlif Low GI rice has a GI of just 40, making it safe and suitable for health diabetics and health conscious people.

Sugarlif Low GI Rice is available in 5 varieties ranging from regular daily use rice to exclusive special occasion Basmati rice.
