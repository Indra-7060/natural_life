#

Have you ever wondered what happens after you stir sugar into your coffee?

Probably not.

Most of us are too busy looking for the biscuit.

But imagine that tiny spoonful beginning a journey through your body.

It’s a bit like boarding an express train.

Some sugars hit the accelerator immediately.

They rush through the system, sending blood glucose climbing in record time.

Others prefer the scenic route.

Steadier.

More measured.

Less dramatic.

The difference isn’t always visible in the sugar bowl.

It certainly isn’t written in giant letters on every packet.

Which is why scientists measure something called the Glycemic Index.

Think of it as a speedometer.

Not for cars.

For carbohydrates.

The lower the speed, the gentler the journey.

That’s why products like Sugarlif undergo independent Glycemic Index testing.

Because when it comes to understanding how food behaves, assumptions are interesting.

Measurements are better.

So the next time you stir a spoonful into your tea, remember…

It may look ordinary.

But every spoonful has a story.

Some simply have more science behind them than others.
