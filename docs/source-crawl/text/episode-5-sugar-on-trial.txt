#

Court is now in session.

The accused?

Sugar.

The charges?

Being responsible for everything from afternoon naps to expanding waistlines.

The courtroom is packed.

Diet trends have arrived.

Fitness influencers are taking notes.

Someone in the back is whispering about keto.

The judge enters.

“Order!”

The prosecution begins dramatically.

“Your Honour… sugar is guilty.”

The judge raises an eyebrow.

“Do you have evidence?”

Silence.

Because science doesn’t convict based on rumours.

It asks better questions.

Which sugar?

How much?

Under what conditions?

How was it tested?

That’s why independent laboratories matter.

They’re the expert witnesses.

They don’t shout.

They don’t speculate.

They simply present the evidence.

For products like Sugarlif, that evidence includes internationally recognised Low GI testing.

Not because science enjoys making life complicated.

Because health deserves something stronger than opinion.

The verdict?

Don’t believe everything you hear in the courtroom of public opinion.

Sometimes the most important witness is the one wearing a lab coat.

Case closed.
