## Natural Life Speciality Pvt. Ltd.

## Our Story

It All Began With One Question…

“Can you make my son eat sweets again?”

It wasn’t a business proposal. It wasn’t the beginning of a company. It wasn’t even the kind of question anyone expects to change their life. It was a mother’s heartfelt plea to Dr. C. K. Nandagopalan, whose young son was living with juvenile diabetes. Sometimes, the biggest journeys begin with the simplest questions. This one certainly did.
Most people would have offered sympathy. Dr. Nandagopalan did something far more difficult—he decided to look for an answer. Not a quick answer. Not an easy answer. A real one. That single question became the spark that would eventually lead to Sugarlif.

[IMG https://natural-life.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/about_img.png]

Ten Years. Countless Questions. One Extraordinary Journey.

If you’re picturing a scientist spending ten years locked inside a laboratory, you’re only partly right. Yes, there were laboratories. There were experiments. There were notebooks overflowing with observations. And there were probably enough cups of forgotten coffee to fill a small pond. But the real journey stretched far beyond the laboratory. Driven by curiosity, Dr. Nandagopalan travelled across South India in search of forgotten food wisdom. He immersed himself in ancient Tamil traditions, studied recipes that had quietly survived for centuries, and spent time living with remote tribal communities whose understanding of food had been passed down through generations rather than written in textbooks. The deeper he explored, the more fascinating the journey became. Modern science offered precision. Ancient wisdom offered perspective. Somewhere between the two lay an answer worth finding. Of course, science has a wonderful sense of humour. Just when you think you’ve solved the puzzle, it politely hands you three new ones. Experiments failed. Ideas were reworked. New questions replaced old assumptions. And every setback quietly whispered, “Try again.” Because that’s what real research looks like.

It’s less like watching fireworks and more like putting together a million-piece jigsaw puzzle… where nobody remembered to include the picture on the box. Yet every experiment, every journey, every conversation and every discovery brought the answer a little closer. After ten remarkable years, the breakthrough finally arrived.

A Different Way to Think About Sugar
The goal was never to invent another artificial sweetener. Or create something that tasted almost like sugar… if you squinted hard enough. The goal was always much simpler. To make real sugar better. The breakthrough was the development of Low GI Sugar—real cane sugar with a lower Glycemic Index, allowing people to enjoy the sweetness they love while making a healthier choice. That breakthrough became the foundation of Sugarlif. Because we believe good food shouldn’t force you to choose between taste and wellbeing. Life already asks enough difficult questions. Your cup of tea shouldn’t be one of them.

One Discovery Led to Another
Once the team had successfully developed Low GI Sugar, another question naturally followed. “If we can do this with sugar… what else is possible?” Curiosity, as it turns out, is wonderfully difficult to switch off. That same spirit of innovation soon expanded into everyday foods such as wheat, rice, jaggery, honey and a growing family of products that people use every single day. After all, healthier living isn’t built on dramatic once-a-year decisions. It’s built on the small choices we make every morning, every afternoon and every evening—usually somewhere between breakfast and “What’s for dinner?” Our mission is beautifully simple. To make the foods people already love, a little better.

Built on Quality. Trusted Around the World.
A good idea deserves good science. And good science deserves independent validation. Sugarlif’s Low GI technology has been evaluated by internationally recognised institutions, including GI Labs, Canada and the Whistler Center for Carbohydrate Research, USA, together with evaluations in Australia and India. Not because we enjoy collecting certificates. They don’t actually make very good wallpaper. But because trust is earned through evidence, consistency and transparency.
Today, every Sugarlif product is manufactured at our dedicated facility in Maharashtra, India, where internationally recognised quality systems guide everything we do—from sourcing ingredients to production, packaging and final quality checks. Our manufacturing facility is certified to the BRC Global Standard for Food Safety, reflecting our commitment to producing food that meets some of the world’s most demanding quality standards. Because when your products become part of someone’s breakfast, dessert or evening cup of tea, “good enough” simply isn’t good enough.

And The Journey Continues…

People often ask us what makes Sugarlif different. The answer isn’t just our products. It’s the question that started everything. “Can you make my son eat sweets again?” That question still sits at the heart of everything we do. It reminds us that behind every product is a person. Behind every innovation is a family. And behind every healthier choice is someone who simply wants to enjoy life without giving up the foods they love. The story of Sugarlif is still being written. With every new product.

## Our Journey

## The Sugarlif Journey

#### “Success is a journey, not a destination. The doing is often more important than the outcome.” — Arthur Ashe

The story of Sugarlif is not about the goal but its all about the journey it took. A journey to achieve a goal which were intimidating, scary, like swimming in sea where the hope of many float in nebulous future of someday. The beginning of the journey was also not less dramatic when mother of a Juvenile Diabetic child made an emotional yet resolute appeal to Dr. Nandagopalan asking, “Can you make my son to eat sweets?”; this emotional appeal triggered the research and development of this revolutionary product. It took them 4 years to achieve a breakthrough with the development of Low GI Sugar. The palpable happiness in the face of the mother and her diabetic child was the Oscar for their effort. What is more pleasing is that they are consuming it till date.

However, like any journey the innovators also faced certain hinderance and heart breaks. In 2011 they joined hands with a Company called Diabliss Consumer Products Pvt Limited co-owned by Mr Ramanathan to manufacture and market the product made of this technology. Between 2011 to 2017 they jointly conducted many tests and clinical study with the product made from this technology.

However, this partnership couldn’t last long as Diabliss didn’t honour the multiple commitments they made, and they parted ways in 2017.

“We now believe Diabliss started manufacturing Low Gi Sugar with some other technology and we have made it very clear to them that we don’t stand any guarantee for their product and its efficacy”.

In the year 2015 Dr. C.K. Nandagopalan and his team in Revolution joined hands with Natural Life and started a brand called Sugarlif and jointly dedicated their focus on getting international validation for their technology and product. They got the product certified in Canada, USA, Australia, India and at the same time committed their efforts to successfully find a way to make this technology scalable to meet the global demand. We feel privileged and proud to be recognised among the pioneers in Low Gi Technology.

Today, this technology is owned by Natural Life Speciality Pvt Ltd. and we believe this is just a beginning of a efficacious and flourishing journey
